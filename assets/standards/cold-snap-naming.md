# Cold Snap™ Naming Convention

**Cold Snap** is the dedicated product line for all Cold Front Forge tabletop, card game (TCG), and card grading accessories.

---

## The Formula

Every product title uses the same structure:

> **Cold Snap [Product Type]** — *[Clear Descriptive Purpose]*

* Keep "Cold Snap" as the branded line name.
* Follow with a simple 1–2 word identifier.
* Add an SEO-friendly description so searchers and makers immediately understand what it does.

## Platform Listing Templates

### Etsy Listing Format
`Cold Snap [Item] | [Key Features / Capacity] | [Compatible Games/Cards] | Cold Front Forge`

* **Example:**
  *Cold Snap Desk Caddy | 3-in-1 Card Prep Station for Toploaders, Semi-Rigids & Sleeves | Pokémon, Sports Cards, MTG*

### MakerWorld / Printables Format
`Cold Snap [Item] – [Functional Description]`

* **Example:**
  *Cold Snap Desk Caddy – 3-in-1 TCG & Card Prep Organizer*
